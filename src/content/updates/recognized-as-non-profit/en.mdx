We have good news: UnitEd has been recognized as a non-profit by our tax
office. Our official status line reads
"Gemeinnützig anerkannt beim Finanzamt Kiel" (Recognized as a non-profit by
the Kiel tax office). You can also find it in our
[imprint](/en/terms/impressum).

## What this means for donors and members

Here is what this can mean for you. Everything below is general information
and may work differently depending on your personal situation.

- Donations to a recognized non-profit (_gemeinnützige Körperschaft_) can
  generally be deducted as special expenses (_Sonderausgaben_) in a German
  income tax return, within statutory limits.
- Proof is a donation receipt on the officially prescribed form
  (_Zuwendungsbestätigung_), issued by us. Keep it. You only need to submit it
  if your tax office asks for it.
- For small amounts, simplified proof such as a bank statement may be enough
  under certain conditions.
- Membership fees can also be deductible, depending on the association's
  purposes.
- Donating time or services (volunteering) is not tax deductible as such.
- If you pay taxes outside Germany, the rules of your own country apply.
  Please check what applies to you there.

For us, it means that we may only use funds for the purposes in our statutes
(_Satzung_), and the tax office checks this regularly.

## How to get a donation receipt

- **Members:** tick the box in the
  [membership form](/en/get-involved/membership/join).
- **All other donors, including donations via GoFundMe:** email
  [hello@uniteducation.net](mailto:hello@uniteducation.net) with your full
  name, postal address, and the amount and date of your donation.
