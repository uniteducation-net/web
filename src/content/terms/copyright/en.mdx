### What you may do – free of charge and without quantity limits

- **Share** – copy and redistribute our material in any format or medium.
- **Adapt** – remix, transform, translate, and build upon the material, for
  example for your own teaching.

## Contributing

Anyone can suggest and contribute content – for example via our community or
on GitHub. To keep our materials freely usable, the following applies to
contributions:

- You confirm that the contribution is your own work or that you hold the
  necessary rights, and
- you make your contribution available under the same license
  (CC BY-NC 4.0).

## Source code

Our source code is publicly available on GitHub
([github.com/uniteducation-net](https://github.com/uniteducation-net)).
Unless otherwise stated in the respective repository, the same principle
applies: free use, modification, and sharing for non-commercial purposes
with attribution; commercial use only with consent.

## Name and logo

The name "UnitEd", our logo, and our other brand marks are **excluded from

an official affiliation, partnership, or endorsement by us.

## Third-party content

Where we use third-party material, the original license indicated at the
source applies. We identify such content accordingly.

## Note on AI and translations

still possible – we welcome any pointers.
