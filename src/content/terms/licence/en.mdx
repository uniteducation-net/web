## Open source

UnitEd is an open-source project. You are welcome to copy, share, and build on
our work.

## Non-commercial use

Usage is allowed for **non-commercial purposes**, as long as you mention
UnitEd as the source. Copy and share — just credit us.

## Commercial use

Commercial use requires our prior written permission. Get in touch before
using UnitEd's work in a commercial context.
