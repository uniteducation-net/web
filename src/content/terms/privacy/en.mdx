## 1. Data protection at a glance

### General information

The following information provides a simple overview of what happens to your
personal data when you visit this website. Personal data is any data that
can be used to identify you personally. For detailed information on data
protection, please refer to this privacy policy below.

### Who is responsible for data collection on this website?

Data processing on this website is carried out by the website operator. You
can find the operator's contact details in the section "Information on the
controller" of this privacy policy.

### How do we collect your data?

On the one hand, your data is collected when you provide it to us. This may,
for example, be data you enter into a form. Other data is collected
automatically by our IT systems, or with your consent, when you visit the
website. This is primarily technical data (e.g. internet browser, operating
system, or time of the page view). This data is collected automatically as
soon as you visit this website.

### What do we use your data for?

Part of the data is collected to ensure the error-free provision of the
website and the services offered. Other data may be used to analyze your
user behavior – currently, however, only with your consent.

### What rights do you have regarding your data?

You have the right at any time to receive information free of charge about
the origin, recipients, and purpose of your stored personal data. You also
have the right to request the rectification or erasure of this data. If you
have given consent to data processing, you may withdraw this consent at any
time with effect for the future. You also have the right, under certain
circumstances, to request the restriction of the processing of your personal
data. Furthermore, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the
competent supervisory authority.

You can contact us at any time about this and any other questions regarding
data protection.

## 4. Data collection on this website

### Cookies

Our website uses only technically necessary cookies. These are required to
provide the basic functions of the website and your personal workspace:

- **session** – an encrypted httpOnly cookie that stores your session and
  your workspace settings (e.g. the connected GitHub repository and the
  selected AI model) for up to 30 days.
- **oauth_state** – a short-lived cookie that protects the GitHub sign-in
  process against attacks and is deleted once sign-in is complete.
- **fu** – a signed httpOnly cookie that counts daily usage of our free AI
  quota and resets every day.

These cookies are necessary for the operation of the website and the
provision of the functions you request. They are set on the basis of § 25(2)
no. 2 TDDDG; the associated data processing is carried out on the basis of
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our
legitimate interest in stable and secure operation).

We do not currently use any analytics or marketing cookies. Should this
change in the future, we will obtain your prior consent via our cookie
banner (§ 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); this consent can be withdrawn at
any time.

## 5. AI-powered features (chatbot)

We offer AI-powered features, in particular a chatbot (AI assistant) that
supports teachers in planning and creating teaching materials.

### Technical implementation via the Vercel AI Gateway

Your requests to the AI assistant are routed through the Vercel AI Gateway
(provider: Vercel Inc., USA) to various AI model providers. We test and
switch the models used in the background in order to achieve the best
possible results; the provider or model answering your request may therefore
vary. In doing so, the content of your inputs and any context data required
for the response (e.g. content from your workspace) is transmitted to the
gateway and the respective model provider. Processing in the USA and
possibly other third countries cannot be excluded; where available, it is
based on the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data
Privacy Framework.

### No training on your data – where possible

We configure our requests so that your data is, as far as possible, not used
to train AI models ("opt-out"). However, individual model providers may
ignore this setting; we have no control over this. **Please therefore do not
enter particularly confidential personal data into the chatbot – in
particular no data about students.**

### Your own API key ("bring your own key")

If you wish, you can provide your own API key from a supported provider
(currently OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or OpenRouter). Your requests then run
through your own contractual relationship with that provider, and you retain
full control over the terms and data processing; the privacy policies of the
respective provider apply in addition. Your key is stored exclusively in
your encrypted session cookie and is used only for your own requests.

Without your own key, we provide a free quota via the Vercel AI Gateway,
which is subject to fair, daily-limited use (see the "Cookies" section).

### Legal bases and notes

Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the AI features you
requested). No automated decision-making with legal effect within the
meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place. Please note that AI-generated content
may be inaccurate or incomplete; review it on your own responsibility before
use (see also our [Terms of Service](/en/terms/terms)).
