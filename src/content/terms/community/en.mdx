## Our principles

UnitEd is run by a volunteer team from all over the world – different
countries, languages, cultures, and time zones. This diversity is exactly
what makes us strong. To ensure everyone feels welcome and safe, these
guidelines apply to all UnitEd community spaces: on GitHub, on our social
media channels, at events, in exchange formats, and wherever people
communicate on behalf of UnitEd.

- **Respect first** – Treat everyone the way you would like to be treated.
  We do not tolerate the disparagement of people or groups.
- **Assume good intent** – Our team comes from many cultural backgrounds;
  misunderstandings happen, including linguistic ones. English is our shared
  working language – be patient with people writing in a language that is
  not their mother tongue.
- **Stay constructive** – Criticism is welcome when it is factual and aims
  to make things better.

## What we encourage

- friendly, respectful language and a benevolent tone,
- constructive feedback on content, ideas, and materials,
- sharing experiences and materials – ideally with attribution of sources,
- supporting new members as they get started,
- consideration for time and cultural differences (we work asynchronously
  across time zones),
- asking friendly questions when something is unclear.

## What we do not tolerate

- harassment, discrimination, and hate speech – for example on the basis of
  origin, skin color, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation,
  disability, or age,
- personal attacks, insults, threats, or deliberate provocation (trolling),
- publishing other people's personal data without their consent (doxxing) –
  this includes names, photos, or contact details,
- spam, unsolicited advertising, and self-promotion without prior
  arrangement,
- unlawful content and copyright infringements,
- deliberately spread misinformation, especially within educational content,
- sexually explicit content or unwanted advances.

## Especially important: protecting learners

Many people in our community work with children and young people. **Never
disclose personal data of students** in community spaces – neither names,
photos, or videos, nor performance or behavioral data. Only share teaching
materials and experiences in anonymized form.

## Content and rights

Only post content for which you hold the necessary rights, and cite your
sources. Contributions you deliberately release for UnitEd are published by
us under our open license (CC BY-NC 4.0) – details on the
[Copyright & Licensing](/en/terms/copyright) page.

## Reporting violations

If you notice a violation of these guidelines, please report it to
[hello@uniteducation.net](mailto:hello@uniteducation.net) – if possible with
a link or screenshot and brief context. We treat reports confidentially,
follow up on every report, and will inform you of the outcome on request.
Misusing the reporting channel (e.g. false accusations) is itself a
violation of these guidelines.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and repetition, violations can have different
consequences:

1. a friendly notice or a warning,
2. removal of content,
3. temporary exclusion from individual or all community spaces,
4. permanent exclusion.

For severe violations (e.g. threats, hate speech, or endangering minors), we
may exclude someone immediately and without warning. We make decisions at
our reasonable discretion and will explain them on request.

## Scope and changes

These guidelines apply to all community spaces operated by UnitEd. We may
adjust them as needed; the current version date is shown at the top of this
page. Our [Terms of Service](/en/terms/terms) apply in addition.
